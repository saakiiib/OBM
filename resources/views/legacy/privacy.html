Privacy notice

How Online Bangla Media handles information submitted through its contribution form.

Information you provide

When you submit a request, we collect the name, email address, listing details and any optional information you supply. Your submission is sent to info@mentosoftware.co.uk for review and response. It is not automatically published.

How information is used

We use submissions to assess directory suggestions, correct information and respond to enquiries. A temporary session cookie helps protect the form against abuse. Hosting and email providers may process requests and technical logs.

Your choices

Do not send sensitive information or identity documents. To ask about your information, request correction or deletion, or raise a privacy concern, contact info@mentosoftware.co.uk.

External websites

Other websites linked from the portal have their own privacy practices.

Submission storage

V4 stores submitted contact details and requests in a restricted administration database and attempts delivery by email. Contact info@mentosoftware.co.uk to request access or deletion. Do not send sensitive personal information.
